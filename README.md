### Hi, I'm Tiago 👋

I'm a **computational physicist**: physicist by training, builder by inclination. Six years of first-principles materials modelling and four years teaching computational physics left me with a habit of turning messy physical problems into code that produces numbers you can trust. I care just as much about explaining the result: I like teaching, and making the lab guides, worked examples and scientific illustrations that go with it.

Away from research, my hobby is making the software I already use do a little more: small improvements, plugins and integrations for existing projects, and the occasional device that shipped without a decent API. If something in my home has Bluetooth and no documentation, it is only a matter of time. Most of it ends up open source, because I learn best in public.

**I'm in the Paris area, I work in English, French and Portuguese, and I'm open to roles in scientific computing, simulation, R&D engineering and data science.** If you're hiring, or just want to talk shop, my inbox is open, and it answers faster than a DFT calculation.

#### What I'm building

- **[Postulo](https://source.tiagoagueda.com/postulo/postulo)**: a self-hosted job application manager, built for the person applying rather than the company hiring. Yes, I built an application to manage my applications. Django, with a plugin system and browser extensions that capture a posting from the page you're on.
- **Plugins and small improvements for existing software**: integrations, add-ons and patches for the self-hosted tools I run at home, such as Home Assistant and Jellyfin, often so that a device or service works locally instead of through its vendor's cloud. Each one starts as "this should take an afternoon" and ends as a repository; the current ones are in [my repositories](https://source.tiagoagueda.com/tiagoagueda?tab=repositories).
- **[tiagoagueda.com](https://www.tiagoagueda.com/en/)**: notes on chaos theory and materials modelling, on a trilingual site whose home page draws the Lorenz attractor live in WebGL ([source](https://source.tiagoagueda.com/tiagoagueda/tiagoagueda-website)).

#### Research and teaching

- **First-principles materials modelling** for energy and electronics, with VASP and ABINIT on HPC clusters: boron defects in amorphous silicon, kesterite photovoltaics, electronic coupling in nanocrystal solids. Three peer-reviewed papers, listed on [my site](https://www.tiagoagueda.com/en/).
- **Teaching**: four years of lab courses in computational physics, simulation and modelling, and electricity and magnetism at the University of Aveiro, for second-year students. I wrote all the lab materials, exercises and assessment criteria.
- **Didactic material**: the part I enjoy most is making the material itself, from lab guides and worked examples to scientific illustrations that make an idea clear before the equations arrive. Some of it is public: [`fiscomp-lab`](https://source.tiagoagueda.com/tiagoagueda/fiscomp-lab) (MATLAB) and [`emag-lab`](https://source.tiagoagueda.com/tiagoagueda/emag-lab) (LaTeX).
